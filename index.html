WELCOME TO LAKEWOOD

A space for every
occasion.

The Lakewood Hotel & Conference Centre is located at 2 Vinton Road in Ormonde, Johannesburg South. Its event spaces support corporate meetings, conferences, weddings and special gatherings, with flexible catering options and an equipped kitchen.

The venue welcomes outside caterers and describes itself as strictly Halaal. Guests can also explore the hotel and on-site facilities as part of their event planning.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

A few helpful answers before you plan your visit or event.

What types of events can I enquire about?

You can enquire about conferences, business meetings, weddings, private functions and other celebrations. Share your event details so the team can advise on suitable options.

Can I request a quotation?

Yes. Use the event enquiry form and include your preferred date, guest count and requirements. The form prepares an enquiry summary for you to review.

Is accommodation available?

Lakewood has hotel accommodation information associated with the venue. Include accommodation needs in your enquiry so availability can be confirmed directly.

How do I confirm availability?

Submit an enquiry with your preferred date and event details. A date is not confirmed until the venue team responds directly.
